How estimates work

Fresh positions (under five minutes), speed and course must align with the canal approach. Commercial type and vessel size increase the likelihood. Windows include travel uncertainty and opening lead time.

AIS lacks air draught, bridge operator decisions and pilot intentions. Confidence is a heuristic score, not a probability. These are estimates, never a confirmed opening schedule.
